ChessNearby Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the ChessNearby app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Vladyslav Lukiianchuk (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device

Location Data Usage

The Application collects your device's precise location to provide the core functionality of finding chess games nearby.

Private Device Location: Your real-time device location is used locally to display the map and calculate distances to nearby challenges. Your live device tracking is never shared with or visible to other users.

Public Challenge Location: When you create a "Challenge" (a request for a game), you select a specific location on the map (e.g., a park, cafe, or library). This specific meeting point coordinates will be visible to other users so they can find the game location.

Control: You can disable location services at any time via your device settings, though this will limit the Application's ability to show games near you.

User Generated Content & Communications

Challenges: When you post a challenge, the details you provide (Time, Rating type, and the selected Meeting Location) become public to allow other players to accept the game.

User Profiles: Basic profile information (Username, Avatar, Rating) is visible to other users in the context of a Challenge.

Chat: Chat functionality becomes available only after a challenge is accepted by both parties. Chat messages are stored securely on our servers (via Supabase and Firebase).

Offline Interaction Warning: The Application is designed to facilitate real-world, offline chess games at a location chosen by users. Please be aware that the Service Provider does not screen users. We advise you to exercise caution and common sense when meeting strangers (e.g., always meet in public places like cafes or parks). The Service Provider is not responsible for user interactions offline.

For a better experience, while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to email address, first name, last name, date of birth, username (nickname), profile photo (avatar), precise location (when enabled), device identifiers, advertising identifiers, chat messages, app activity, and usage data. The information that the Service Provider request will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.
