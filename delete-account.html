Delete Account – ChessNearby

This page explains how users of the ChessNearby application can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

If you wish to delete your ChessNearby account and all associated data, please send an email request to:

takate.app@gmail.com

Please include the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"

To help us identify your account, include the email address or user ID used to sign in to the application.

Data that will be deleted

Upon processing your request, the following data will be permanently deleted from our active systems (Supabase and Firebase):

  • User account and authentication records
  • Profile information (username, avatar, rating)
  • Chat messages and conversation history
  • Challenge data created by the user
  • Location-related data associated with challenges

Data retention

Some information may be retained for a limited period if required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. Any retained data will be removed within a reasonable timeframe, typically within 30 days.

Additional information

Uninstalling the application will stop further data collection, but it will not automatically delete your account or stored data.

For more details about how we handle personal information, please review our Privacy Policy.
