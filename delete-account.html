Delete Account – ChessNearby

This page explains how users of the ChessNearby application can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

You have two options to delete your account and data:

Option 1: Delete within the App (Recommended)

You can delete your account directly inside the application without sending an email request:

  1. Open the ChessNearby app.
  2. Go to the Settings screen.
  3. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) usually located at the top corner.
  4. Select "Delete Account" from the menu options.
  5. Confirm your choice. This will immediately delete your account and data.

Option 2: Request via Email

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot access it, please send an email request to:

takate.app@gmail.com

Please include the subject line:
"Account Deletion Request"

To help us identify your account, please include the email address or user ID used to sign in to the application.

Data that will be deleted

Upon processing your request, the following data will be permanently deleted from our active systems (Supabase and Firebase):

  • User account and authentication records
  • Profile information (username, avatar, rating)
  • Chat messages and conversation history
  • Challenge data created by the user
  • Location-related data associated with challenges

Data retention

Some information may be retained for a limited period if required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. Any retained data will be removed within a reasonable timeframe, typically within 30 days.

Additional information

Uninstalling the application will stop further data collection, but it will not automatically delete your account or stored data. You must use one of the methods above.

For more details about how we handle personal information, please review our Privacy Policy.
